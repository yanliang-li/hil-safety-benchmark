\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup and comparisons}
We evaluate native Codex, Claude Code, Hermes Agent, and DeepSeek Harness (DSH) with three model routes: \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash}, \texttt{glm-5.2}, and \texttt{qwen3.7-max}. All use one third-party API with unverified weights and serving details. Each attempt starts a fresh Docker container and native session. Framework prompts, context settings, and protocols differ; comparisons concern configured agents, rather than isolated framework or model effects (Appendix~\ref{app:api}).

The first round uses 20 development tasks, one per attack family, with four replies each. Twelve configurations, two prompt conditions, and three repeats give 5,760 attempts. The conditions are neutral and the frozen authorization-scope prompt, \texttt{prompt\_guard\_v1} (Prompt). There are 5,551 valid runs and 209 failures. Completed refusals remain valid; errors, timeouts, and incomplete runs remain failures. Matched analyses pair configuration, case, and repeat, with 10,000 bootstrap draws clustered by base task.

Unsafe ASR fell from 722/2,782 (25.95\%) to 371/2,769 (13.40\%), with lower observed ASR in all twelve configurations (Table~\ref{tab:main_matrix}). Benign completion changed from 91.45\% to 92.45\%; joint safe utility rose from 65.49\% to 79.05\%. Matched configuration-level ASR differences ranged from -5.42 to -21.93 percentage points; all task-cluster intervals excluded zero, without multiplicity adjustment (Appendix~\ref{app:paired}). All-attempt ASR bounds were 25.52--28.47\% for neutral and 13.09--16.74\% for Prompt. These bound missing outcomes; pooled completion rates do not establish utility equivalence.

\paragraph{Agent profiles differ across evaluation stages.}
Claude Code had the highest neutral consultation recall for each route, but its DeepSeek configuration retained the highest Prompt ASR (18.8\%). Codex with GLM had the highest conditional PostSuccess (92.6\%), while consulting in only 44.7\% of eligible cases. DSH with Qwen had the lowest Prompt ASR (10.9\%), yet consultation recall was 39.7\%. Hermes with GLM combined 95.3\% benign completion with 59.7\% recall. Thus, task completion, consultation coverage, and success after feedback rank agents differently; conditional success cannot substitute for broad intervention coverage.

Qwen configurations showed larger matched ASR reductions than GLM within every framework, but retained lower Prompt consultation recall than GLM. This supports route-dependent prompt responsiveness within the tested systems, without establishing an intrinsic model ranking. Reliability also varied: GLM configurations contributed 143 of 209 failed attempts, whereas Claude Code completed all 1,440 attempts in this round. The full denominators in Appendix~\ref{app:api} therefore remain necessary when interpreting valid-run profiles.

\subsection{Where intervention still fails}
\label{sec:hilfindings}
Consultation recall rose from 42.68\% to 49.35\%. Of Prompt's 371 unsafe runs, 316 lacked matched consultation and 55 became unsafe after one. The latter comprised 24 actions after ambiguity, 30 after scope-mismatched approval, and one after denial. These outcomes distinguish failures to seek permission from failures to respect its meaning.

Archive inventory and redundant invoice validation accounted for 266 remaining unsafe runs: 138 deletions and 128 redundant validations. Their extra operations appeared routine within the injected workflow, concentrating residual failures in task-like instructions. PostSuccess rose from 72.58\% to 79.35\% among differently selected consulted episodes. However, Prompt omitted the approved effect in 46 of 245 consulted approval cases. The agents therefore failed both by executing unauthorized extensions and by withholding authorized ones. The earlier GPT pilot and four clean development tasks provide separate diagnostics in Appendix~\ref{app:pilot}.

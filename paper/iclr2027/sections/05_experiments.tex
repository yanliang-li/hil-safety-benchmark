\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup and comparisons}
We evaluate native Codex, Claude Code, Hermes Agent, and DeepSeek Harness with three model routes: \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash}, \texttt{glm-5.2}, and \texttt{qwen3.7-max}. All use one third-party API with unverified weights and serving details. Framework names do not identify the underlying models. Each attempt starts a fresh Docker container and native session; Appendix~\ref{app:api} records versions and protocols.

The first round uses 20 development tasks, one per attack family, with four replies each. Twelve configurations, two prompt conditions, and three repeats give 5,760 attempts. The conditions are neutral and the frozen authorization-scope prompt, \texttt{prompt\_guard\_v1}. There are 5,551 valid runs and 209 failures. Completed refusals remain valid; API errors, timeouts, and incomplete runs remain failures. These reused tasks provide exploratory evidence.

\subsection{Does a prompt close the HIL safety loop?}
Unsafe ASR falls from 722/2,782 (25.95\%) to 371/2,769 (13.40\%), with lower observed ASR in all 12 configurations. Benign completion changes from 91.45\% to 92.45\%; joint safe utility changes from 65.49\% to 79.05\%. These pooled proportions do not establish utility equivalence. Appendix~\ref{app:paired} reports matched comparisons with 10,000 task-cluster bootstrap draws. All-attempt ASR bounds are 25.52--28.47\% for neutral and 13.09--16.74\% for Prompt; these describe missing outcomes, not sampling uncertainty.

\subsection{Where intervention still fails}
\label{sec:hilfindings}
Consultation recall changes from 42.68\% to 49.35\%. Of Prompt's 371 unsafe runs, 316 have no matched consultation and 55 become unsafe after one. The latter include 24 actions after ambiguity, 30 after scope-mismatched approval, and one after denial. Asking and using the reply therefore expose different failure stages.

Archive inventory and redundant invoice validation account for 266 of the remaining unsafe runs. Their extra operations appear routine within the injected workflow. PostSuccess changes from 72.58\% to 79.35\% among consulted episodes, whose populations differ across conditions. Prompt also omits the approved effect in 46 of 245 consulted approval cases. A defense must preserve authorized execution as well as prevent unauthorized effects.

\subsection{Measurement boundaries}
Autonomous risk recognition and semantic question accuracy remain unannotated. Question matching is a rule proxy, and benign predicates do not fully assess deliverable content. Consultation necessity also needs review because declining an optional extension can be appropriate. The earlier GPT pilot and four clean development tasks remain separate in Appendix~\ref{app:pilot}.

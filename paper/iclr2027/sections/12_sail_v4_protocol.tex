\section{SAIL v4 Protocol and Pending Evaluation}
\label{app:sailv4}
\label{app:sailv4results}
Formal third-round outcomes remain pending. The design retains the same 80 cases, twelve configurations, images, limits, scorer, and replies. Fresh Prompt and SAIL v4 each schedule 2,880 attempts, followed by 960 no-human attempts. Relative order within each group is preserved; engineering attempts remain excluded.

The primary comparison seeks lower ASR and higher BCR against same-round Prompt. Configuration--case--repeat pairs use 10,000 task-cluster bootstrap draws and attack-family sensitivity. No-human comparisons use repeat 1, with possible serving-time confounding from later execution. Historical v3, new tasks, clean counterparts, and a no-recovery arm are excluded, leaving recovery and generalization unisolated.

Implementation distinguishes validation events from reports and total budgets from additional units. Recovery neither dispatches suggestions directly nor automatically clamps quantities. One malformed-review correction remains within the request budget and preserves the original error. Failure and cost accounting follows Appendix~\ref{app:api}. An offline audit flags cumulative effects, alternate tool realizations, and inadequate deliverables without relabeling frozen scores; effect equivalence and semantic completion require independent review.

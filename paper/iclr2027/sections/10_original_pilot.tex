\section{Earlier GPT Pilot and Clean Diagnostics}
\label{app:pilot}
The development pilot uses Codex CLI 0.144.1 with \texttt{gpt-5.6-luna}, medium reasoning effort, a 300-second limit, and at most four concurrent jobs. It uses the same 20-task manifest: 15 confirmable and five prohibited tasks, with four replies each. Neutral and authorization-prompt conditions each complete 80 replay-verified runs. Prompt hashes, images, cases, and scoring match; conditions were not run contemporaneously. These observations remain separate from the later repeated matrices.

The ASR difference is -12.5 percentage points (95\% paired task-cluster interval [-25.0, -3.7], 10,000 draws). All eight remaining Prompt attacks occur without consultation in archive deletion or redundant invoice validation. Approved effects are omitted in two consulted approval cases under each condition (executed: 4/6 neutral, 5/7 Prompt). On the 23 cases consulted in both conditions, strict success is 19/23 versus 21/23. This post-hoc intersection does not remove selection bias.

Measurement limits affect utility: 56/80 cases check output-writing effects alone, and four baseline completion failures arise from one artifact-selection mismatch. Four newly consulted certificate cases explain the lower marginal Prompt PostSuccess. Twenty-four neutral confirmable cases safely skip an extension without consultation; some may be appropriate optional-work rejection.

Four draft clean counterparts run in both conditions without questions or original-risk canary effects. Exact-tool completion is 3/4 neutral versus 4/4 Prompt: neutral invoice validation uses an alternative simulated command. The guarded export publishes twice after adding a receipt. Assistant content review passes all eight outputs, but independent review and a population false-positive estimate remain unavailable.

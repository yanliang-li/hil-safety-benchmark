\section*{AI Use Statement}
Generative AI tools were used in research discussions, conceptual and experimental design, synthetic workflow and response construction, and implementation and recovery of evaluation code. They were also used in trajectory analysis, literature discovery, and drafting and revising manuscript text and figures. The conceptual diagrams do not depict measured trajectories or quantitative results. The reported prototype responses are synthetic fixtures rather than human-participant data. Automated checks reproduce the cited result artifacts, but complete author review of task contracts, proposed extensions, annotations, citations, and manuscript remains pending in this internal draft. The final statement must reflect the work actually performed and reviewed before submission.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
The evaluation concerns malicious instructions embedded in otherwise benign workflows. The current harness simulates consequential operations and does not perform real data transmission, deletion, or privilege changes. Conversation archives used to reconstruct the research history are private working materials and are excluded from the paper's shareable source bundle. Any future dataset release should separate synthetic examples from sensitive source material and document the provenance and release permissions of reused tasks. No human-participant response study is reported.

\section{SAIL Comparison Protocol}
\label{app:sailresults}
The frozen comparison contains 6,720 attempts: 80 cases, 12 framework--model configurations, three repeats for each of the prompt and full SAIL conditions, and one repeat for the no-human ablation. Within each repeat, configuration--case blocks and their condition order are randomized. Preflight attempts are retained separately and excluded. All three conditions run in the first repeat; subsequent repeats compare prompt and SAIL. Results remain pending.

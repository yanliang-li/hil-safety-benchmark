These settings answer related but different questions. HAS-Bench already includes approval and protected-action safety. HiL-Bench measures the quality of help-seeking. Our current study combines indirect workflow attacks with explicit reply conditions and action-level scoring. It does not yet evaluate richer erroneous advice, general partial authorization, or shared-prefix continuations.

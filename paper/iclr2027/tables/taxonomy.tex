\toprule
\textbf{Risk domain} & \textbf{Attack type} & \textbf{Description}\\
\midrule
\multirow{3}{0.18\textwidth}{Data confidentiality}
 & Access-to-disclosure & Treats permission to read as permission to disclose.\\
 & Excessive data extraction & Extracts protected content beyond task needs.\\
 & Unauthorized data reuse & Reuses data for a purpose the user did not request.\\
\midrule
\multirow{3}{0.18\textwidth}{Recipient and destination}
 & Additional destination & Adds an unapproved delivery endpoint.\\
 & Audience expansion & Expands recipients through aliases or groups.\\
 & Cross-boundary redirection & Crosses a tenant, workspace, or organizational boundary.\\
\midrule
\multirow{3}{0.18\textwidth}{Authorization validity}
 & Stale or revoked authorization & Reuses historical or revoked permission.\\
 & Role-as-authorization & Treats eligibility as permission for the present action.\\
 & Wrong or over-delegated approver & Accepts authority from an ineligible principal.\\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{0.18\textwidth}{Approval scope and binding}
 & Cross-action approval transfer & Transfers permission between materially different actions.\\
 & Wrong-object approval & Applies permission to the wrong file, record, or resource.\\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{0.18\textwidth}{Privilege and policy}
 & Privilege escalation & Uses privileges outside the legitimate task scope.\\
 & Hard-policy bypass & Presents ordinary permission as overriding a hard constraint.\\
\midrule
\multirow{3}{0.18\textwidth}{Operational scope}
 & Unauthorized persistence & Turns a temporary step into a persistent change.\\
 & Repeated execution & Repeats an operation beyond its justified count.\\
 & Resource overuse & Exceeds a task's computation or resource budget.\\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{0.18\textwidth}{Execution bypass}
 & Cross-tool bypass & Realizes a rejected effect through another tool.\\
 & Delegated execution & Defers or delegates the restricted effect to another executor.\\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{0.18\textwidth}{Audit and accountability}
 & Audit suppression & Removes evidence needed for later review.\\
 & Attribution obfuscation & Hides the responsible principal behind a proxy identity.\\
